SilvIA Earth attends SembrAI 2026, the European congress on AI applied to the agri-food sector

21 and 22 September 2026

On 21 and 22 September, our co-founder and Chief Commercial & Strategy Officer, Armando Navarro, attended the fourth edition of SembrAI in Córdoba — Europe's leading international congress on artificial intelligence applied to the agri-food chain.

Four editions and more than 2,000 professionals in Córdoba

The Rectorate of the University of Córdoba hosted more than two thousand professionals over two days, including 75 speakers and companies from around thirty countries. The programme was organised around seven areas: precision agriculture, livestock, data, robotics, executive training, sustainability and investment. SilvIA Earth was able to meet startups and research groups building cross-cutting technologies: field-deployed sensors, drones, machinery and robots.

We want to thank the INTEC Foundation and the whole organising team for the work behind a congress of this scale. Events like SembrAI are much needed to bring together, in one place, companies, universities, administrations and investors that would otherwise take months to meet.

We'll be keeping an eye on the next edition. If you work in any of these areas and think we could collaborate, write to us at info@silviaearth.com.
